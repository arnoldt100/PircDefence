Below are some typical positions found in the Pirc.

\section{The Dragon Pirc}

\newchessgame[id=TypicalPositionDragonPirc,
initialfigure=\thefigurecnt,
moveid=12b,
setwhite={kg1,qf3,rf1,ra1,nc3,bd3,be3,pa2,pb2,pc2,pe4,pf4,pg2,ph3},
addblack={kg8,qa5,rf8,ra8,nc6,nf6,bg7,pa7,pb7,pd6,pe7,pf7,pg6,ph7},
storefen=TPDragonPirc]

\chessboard[inverse,showmover=true]
\lfigure

The position arises from the Austrian Attack. Black plays
c5 and cxd4, or White plays dxc5.
